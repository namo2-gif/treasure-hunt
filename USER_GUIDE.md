# Part 1: The 3-Person Pilot Walkthrough (Step-by-Step Test)

To test all mechanics with **3 people (or 3 browser tabs/phones)**, you will use:
- **Phone 1 / Browser 1:** Admin Controller (Headquarters at `/hq`)
- **Phone 2 / Browser 2:** Player 1 — **Captain** (e.g. `S0101` from Team `T01`)
- **Phone 3 / Browser 3:** Player 2 — **Teammate** (e.g. `S0102` from Team `T01`)
*(Optional: Use an incognito tab for Player 3 `S0103` if you want all 3 teammates present, but 2 players + 1 admin is enough to test everything!)*

---

### Step 0: Ensure Test Mode & Clean Slate
1. Go to [`https://<your-domain>/hq`](https://treasure-hunt-techspardha2k26.vercel.app/hq).
2. If prompted, log in with an admin credential (from your `SEED_ADMINS`, e.g., `asha` / `Pw1`).
3. Under the main clock, verify the phase is **`setup`**.
4. *(Optional)* At the bottom of `/hq`, type `RESET` into the box and click **Reset Event Data** to ensure clean testing scores.

---

### Step 1: Login & The "Stand By" Phase
1. **Player 1 (Captain):**
   - Open `/login` on Phone 2.
   - Enter Student ID: `S0101`.
   - Enter Code: (find `S0101`'s code in `codes.csv` generated by `seed.ts`, e.g., `ABCDEF`).
   - Click **Log In**.
   - **What they see:** A clean dark screen: *"Stand by — Round 1 has not started yet."*
2. **Player 2 (Teammate):**
   - Open `/login` on Phone 3.
   - Enter Student ID: `S0102` and code.
   - **What they see:** Same *"Stand by"* screen with *"Agent"* badge instead of *"Captain"*.

---

### Step 2: Test Round 1 (The Website Riddle Quiz)
1. **Admin (Phone 1 / HQ):**
   - In `/hq`, click the green primary button: **"Start Round 1"**.
   - The event clock begins ticking down (e.g. 30:00).
2. **Players (Phones 2 & 3):**
   - Within 5 seconds (or on reload), the screen automatically turns into the **Campus Riddles** quiz!
   - **Teammate (Phone 3):** Notices a blue banner: *"👀 You are viewing in read-only mode. Only your captain can submit answers."* All options are viewable so the team can discuss together.
   - **Captain (Phone 2):** Radio buttons and **"Submit Riddle"** buttons are active!
   - Captain picks an answer for Riddle #1 and submits.
   - **Feedback:** Instantly shows `"Correct."` in green (or `"Wrong."` in red).
   - The score counter updates to *"1 of 12 solved"*.

---

### Step 3: End Round 1 & Select Teams for Round 2
1. **Admin (Phone 1 / HQ):**
   - In `/hq`, click **"End Round 1"**.
   - Phase changes to **`r1_closed`**.
   - Players' screens immediately switch to: *"Round 1 is over. Results are being counted."*
2. **Admin Selection Page:**
   - In the top navigation, click **Selection** (or go to `/hq/selection`).
   - You see a ranked table of teams based on who scored points the fastest.
   - Team `T01` is at the top with points!
   - Ensure the checkbox next to `T01` is **checked** (Qualified).
   - Click the navigation link back to **Dashboard**.
   - The green primary button now says: **"Start Round 2"**. Click it!

---

### Step 4: Round 2 QR Scan (The Campus Race)
1. **Players (Phones 2 & 3):**
   - Both phones automatically switch to: *"Run 1: find a QR code — Scan any QR code around campus with your phone camera."*
2. **Test Scanning Beacons:**
   - Admin goes to `/hq/qr` to see the active QR locations.
   - Click on the link for Location #1 (e.g., Library, token `abc...`).
   - **Captain (Phone 2):** Opens `/s/<token>` for Location #1.
     - Confirmation screen appears: *"Registered at Library. You are piece 1 of 3."*
     - Click **"Go to Home Screen"**.
   - **Teammate (Phone 3):** Tries to open the **same** Location #1 link:
     - 🛑 **What happens:** Screen says *"Registration Failed — A teammate already registered here. Find another location."* (Anti-clustering rule works!)
   - **Teammate (Phone 3):** Now opens Location #2 (e.g., Campus Green, token `xyz...`).
     - Confirmation screen appears: *"Registered at Campus Green. You are piece 2 of 3."*
     - Click **"Go to Home Screen"**.

---

### Step 5: Round 2 Teaser Puzzle (The "Split & Unite" Mechanic)
1. **Players (Phones 2 & 3):**
   - As soon as the teammates scan, their home screens turn into the **Teaser Screen**.
   - **Captain:** Sees piece #1 text (e.g. *"THE"*).
   - **Teammate:** Sees piece #2 text (e.g. *"CLOCK"*).
   - Each player only has their own puzzle piece on their phone! They must shout or talk to each other to combine the pieces into the full phrase.
2. **Captain Solves Teaser:**
   - Captain enters the combined phrase into the answer box and clicks **"Submit answer"**.
   - If wrong: *"Not quite. Keep talking to your team."* (Unlimited tries, zero points deducted).
   - If correct: The teaser unlocks and transitions to **Poster Questions**!

---

### Step 6: Poster Questions (Collaborative Answering)
1. **Teammate (Phone 3):**
   - Sees *"Your questions"* with a location: *"Find the poster at Library: 'What year was the cornerstone laid?'"*
   - The teammate goes to that location, reads the poster, and reports the answer back to the Captain.
2. **Captain (Phone 2):**
   - Sees the **Captain Board**: Has multiple-choice options (`a`, `b`, `c`, `d`), but **not the poster stem!**
   - Captain selects the option reported by the teammate and submits.
   - Result: `"Correct."` or `"Wrong. One try left."` (Tries countdown accurately).

---

### Step 7: Round 2 Hard End, Break & Round 3 Winner Check
1. **Admin (Phone 1 / HQ):**
   - In `/hq`, click **"End Round 2"**.
   - Phase changes to **`r2_break`**.
   - Players see: *"Break — Round 2 is over. Round 3 is coming."*
   - Once the break timer expires (or admin clicks to proceed), click **"Open Round 3"**.
   - The system automatically selects the **Top 5 teams** based on Round 2 points!
2. **Players (Top 5 Team T01):**
   - Both Captain and Teammate see the **Round 3 Decryption Screen** with an enciphered clue.
   - Any team member can enter the password.
   - Enter a wrong password 5 times $\rightarrow$ Screen temporarily locks for 30 seconds with a countdown timer!
   - Enter the correct password (e.g. from `/hq/round3`).
   - 🏆 **VICTORY SCREEN:**
     - The screen explodes with gold:
     - Large Team ID: **`T01`**
     - *"Go to the room and show this to the organiser."*
     - Room Number: **`Room 204`**
     - Secret Key Info displayed.
3. **Admin Verification at the Room Door (`/hq/round3`):**
   - The organiser stationed at Room 204 has `/hq/round3` open on their phone.
   - The moment Team T01 submits the password, the **Official Winning Claim Banner** lights up in gold:
     - *"Official Winning Claim: Team T01 (Submitted by S0101 at 12:45 PM) — Room 204 — Check Team ID at Door"*.
   - The organiser checks the student's phone, confirms Team T01, and hands them the trophy!

# Part 2: Complete System Features & Admin Superpowers

| Feature / Page | What It Does | When To Use It |
|---|---|---|
| **Pausable Event Clock (`/hq`)** | One global clock in Postgres (`event_now_ms`). All deadlines (R1 end, Run 2 open, run deadlines, hard end, break) exist in event time. | If you pause the hunt for 5 minutes, every single team's timer freezes and resumes with zero lost time. |
| **Add Time (`/hq`)** | Shifts all deadlines forward by $X$ minutes simultaneously. | Use this if campus Wi-Fi drops for 4 minutes. Just add 4 minutes! |
| **Emergency Pause (`/hq`)** | Immediately freezes all participant actions with code `PAUSED`. | If there is an accident, sudden weather emergency, or organizer briefing. |
| **Make Captain (`/hq/teams`)** | Swaps team captaincy from Student A to Student B in a single atomic database transaction. | If the captain's phone battery dies, their screen breaks, or they need to leave. |
| **Reset Code (`/hq/teams`)** | Generates a fresh 6-character login code, hashes it with bcrypt, and reveals it to the admin in a dialog. | If a student forgets their slip of paper or enters the wrong code repeatedly. |
| **Unlock Login (`/hq/teams`)** | Clears `failed_logins` and resets lockout delays to zero. | If a mischievous student got locked out by guessing codes. |
| **Mark Absent (`/hq/teams`)** | Marks a student absent and lets the admin pick an active teammate to take over all their puzzle pieces and questions. | If a member drops out. Team size $N$ and point formulas remain completely balanced. |
| **Void Question (`/hq/interventions`)** | Voids an ambiguous or damaged question for a single team or all teams. Awards full points for points lost so far. | If a physical poster is vandalized, blown away by wind, or has a typo. |
| **Admin Register (`/hq/interventions`)** | Bypasses the 12-person QR capacity limit to manually place a student at a location. | If a student's camera lens is broken or a QR tag is temporarily inaccessible. |
| **Skip Teaser (`/hq/interventions`)** | Marks a team's teaser puzzle as skipped and unlocks their poster questions immediately. | If a team is mathematically blocked or stuck on a puzzle. |
| **Reset Attempts (`/hq/interventions`)** | Deletes attempt records for a specific question or riddle. | If a phone glitch accidentally double-submitted or an organizer grants a retry. |
| **Printable QR Sheet (`/hq/qr/print`)** | Browser-native printable layout that renders 4 high-resolution QR beacons per A4 sheet with location labels. | Print these on cardstock and tape them up around campus. |
| **Live Standings & Exports (`/hq/logs`)** | Real-time immutable audit log + 1-click CSV downloads for registrations, attempts, and standings. | For post-event audits, prizes, and dispute resolution. |

# Part 3: Real-World "Story Mode" Scenarios

Here is how the app protects you against chaotic campus surprises:

### Story 1: "The Wet Poster"
- **What happens:** During Round 2, rain ruins Poster #4 at the Fountain. Teams cannot read the riddle question.
- **What admin does:** Admin opens `/hq/interventions`, enters Question ID `4`, types Reason: *"Fountain poster ruined by rain"*, and clicks **Void Question**.
- **What players see:** Any team assigned that question sees its status turn to `Voided`. Teams that already answered wrong receive full points automatically without penalties, and their run completion is re-evaluated immediately.

### Story 2: "The Dead Battery"
- **What happens:** Team T04's captain phone dies at 20% into Round 1. Only the captain can submit answers.
- **What admin does:** Admin goes to `/hq/teams`, searches `T04`, finds teammate `S0402`, and clicks **Make Captain**.
- **What players see:** Within 5 seconds, the new student's phone updates: their badge turns to **Captain** in gold, and active submission radio buttons appear on their screen!

### Story 3: "The Campus Wi-Fi Blackout"
- **What happens:** At minute 15 of Round 1, the university Wi-Fi resets for 6 minutes. Nobody can load pages.
- **What admin does:** When Wi-Fi restores, the admin goes to `/hq`, types `6` into **Add time**, and clicks **Add time**.
- **What players see:** Players refresh their browsers. Instead of Round 1 having expired, their countdown clock has expanded by exactly 6 minutes! No database rows were corrupted, and no progress was lost.

### Story 4: "The Double Scan Stampede"
- **What happens:** Two rival teammates from Team T07 rush to the same QR code at the Library and scan it at the exact same fraction of a second.
- **What happens in the app:** The database executes atomic row-level locking (`FOR UPDATE`). The first student is registered and gets piece #1. The second student's screen flashes red: *"A teammate already registered here. Find another location."* Zero over-capacity errors, zero duplicate assignments.

### Story 5: "The Photo-Sharing Cheat Attempt"
- **What happens:** A lazy player takes a photo of a QR beacon and texts it to a friend in their dorm room.
- **What happens in the app:** The friend scans the photo and gets a piece. BUT:
  1. Round 2 requires solving the teaser (which requires all teammates' pieces combined).
  2. Poster questions are physically distributed: the teammate must tell the captain what is written on the poster at their assigned beacon location.
  3. The captain sees the options, but **not the question stem**! The team is physically forced to walk to the posters and coordinate.
